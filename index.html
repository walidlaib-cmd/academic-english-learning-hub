What is Academic English?

Definition: Academic English is the formal, precise, and evidence-based use of English to understand, discuss, and communicate ideas in higher education.

This course develops the language skills first-year students need to study Economic, Commercial, and Management Sciences. It connects academic vocabulary, register, grammar, and clear writing with core ideas such as scarcity, opportunity cost, markets, money, national output, management, banking, and marketing.

Work through each unit’s lecture text, then use its short questions and applied case to explain an idea in your own words. The searchable exam-reading library contains the text extracted from the supplied exam PDF; its pages are presented on this site rather than as a PDF download.
